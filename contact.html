Before you get in touch

Common questions.

Is there any cost to request a proposal?

No. Proposals and discovery calls are free and come with no obligation. When you do engage resources, there are zero setup fees.

What should I prepare for the discovery call?

A rough picture of your technology stack, the roles you're struggling to fill, and how many people you might need. We'll help shape the rest.

Will my information be kept confidential?

Yes. We match and brief resources privately, and your details are used only to respond to your enquiry.
