FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything technology leaders usually ask before adding a KAJARA resource to their team.

Ask us directly
What is IT staff augmentation?

IT staff augmentation adds dedicated technical professionals to your existing team. They work full-time on your projects and report to your managers, while a partner like KAJARA handles sourcing, contracts, compliance and payroll.

How is KAJARA different from an outsourcing agency?

We are not an outsourced agency, software development firm or managed service provider. We don't take over your projects. Our resources join your team and follow your direction, your code reviews and your deployment cycles.

Who owns the code our resources write?

You do. All intellectual property, generated source code and associated royalties remain 100% owned by your organisation.

Do we choose the developers?

Yes. We shortlist vetted candidates against your requirements. You review résumés, run technical interviews and make the final selection.

How does pricing work?

You pay one transparent, predictable rate for each dedicated full-time resource. There are no setup fees and no revenue share.

Can we scale the team up or down?

Yes. You can grow or reduce your team as project demand shifts, without the long-term commitment of permanent hiring.
