FAQ

Service questions.

Ask about your stack
Do you take on fixed-scope projects?

No. KAJARA provides dedicated, full-time resources who work under your direction. If you need a project delivered, your embedded team delivers it, with you in control of scope, priorities and releases.

Can a resource use our existing tools and processes?

Yes. Resources join your sprint cycles, standups, repositories and roadmap tools, and follow your coding standards and deployment approvals.

Can we mix CMS and cloud roles in one team?

Yes. Many teams combine CMS developers with a DevOps or cloud engineer so content, code and infrastructure move together. Tell us your mix and we'll match each role.

Which technologies do your resources cover?

Ecommerce (Adobe Commerce, Shopify, WooCommerce), CMS (WordPress, Drupal, Joomla), development (PHP, Python, Laravel, .NET, Node.js, Angular), cloud (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud) and DevOps (IaC, CI/CD, FinOps).
